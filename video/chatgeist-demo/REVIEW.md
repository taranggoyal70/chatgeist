# Review

## Creative calls

- Built **The Room Speaks**: result first, one real product state carried through the film, actual Chatgeist voice as the only narration.
- Did not build the architecture-first endless zoom or the conventional before/after split-screen alternatives.
- Used the product's dark observatory only for real product and claim scenes; tension, architecture, and end card move to a light ground so the film does not stay in one dark world.
- The simulator remains visibly labeled and the flow shot says it must be replaced with real Twitch capture before submission.

## What was checked

- HyperFrames check: zero lint, runtime, layout, motion, contrast, craft, audio, or snapshot warnings after fixes.
- WCAG contrast: 26/26 text checks passed.
- Review sheets inspected: 14 beat frames, all 6 seam strips, and the 360 px phone sheet.
- Sound was measured by the framework and the source MP3 was separately verified at 14.616 seconds; it was not judged by listening in this review.

## Review notes

- P0 fixed: text floor warnings, intentional screenshot overflow, two static stretches, and end-card scale.
- Weakest shot: the control-room flow is dense at phone width. It establishes the real product but should be replaced in the final edit by real Twitch capture focused on the overlay, not by more UI text.
- Kept on purpose: the claim scene uses a dark ring because it repeats the actual Chatgeist observatory/orbit motif rather than adding unrelated decorative glow.
- Required before submission: replace the provisional simulator beat with real Twitch footage while keeping the live/simulated label truthful.
