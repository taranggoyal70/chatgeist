# Chatgeist Storylines

## Recommended — The Room Speaks

**Device / form:** Result first; one frame repeatedly rebuilds itself.

Open on the real split Chatgeist answer already working, then pull back through the verified control room, summoning state, source-backed transformation, architecture proof, and Codex build evidence. The same two spirit silhouettes carry the eye through each world so the product—not abstract motion—remains the hero.

**First two seconds:** Two living factions flank the grounded sentence: “The room’s split on forgiveness.”

**Beats:** result → fast chat loses its shape → summon → ask → grounded split + product voice → architecture/Codex proof → claim → end card.

**Look:** product screenshots fill the frame; dark observatory palette belongs to the product; warm memory-gold marks evidence and the final claim.

**Sound:** actual Chatgeist product voice is the only spoken sound. No music source is added in the provisional cut.

## Alternative — Signal Through The Machine

**Device / form:** Endless zoom through the actual data flow.

The camera dives from a chat message into the bounded evidence window, through the structured output, and out through the 3D spirit. Strong technical story, weaker human immediacy.

## Alternative — Before Chatgeist / After Chatgeist

**Device / form:** Split screen collapsing into one.

One side shows chat fragments scrolling past; the other condenses them into a grounded answer with visible people. Clear, but more conventional and less cinematic than result-first.
